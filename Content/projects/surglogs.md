Healthcare-compliance software used daily by 787+ ambulatory surgery centres across
the US, with 91k+ monthly sessions. I am the sole technical owner of the iOS platform.

### Architecture

196k+ lines of native Swift across 29 modules, including 14 independent SPM packages,
built as MVVM + Coordinator. I drove the platform-wide migration to SwiftUI,
Swift Concurrency and Swift 6, and integrated Firebase, AWS Cognito, PostHog and Sentry.

### Delivery

439 App Store releases across five major versions, at 99.9% crash-free sessions.
6,500+ monthly active users, with 91% growth over the last 20 months.

### Quality infrastructure

I built the CI/CD platform from the ground up: 18,000+ pipelines running build and unit
testing, SwiftLint, SonarQube, static security and complexity analysis, license auditing
and parallel acceptance testing. The automated suite covers 2,270 unit tests and
1,780 BDD scenarios (XCUITest + Cucumber) across 6 parallel suites, gating every
merge request.

### Leadership

Since 2022 I have also led a cross-functional feature team of 5–8 engineers as Team Lead
and Scrum Master — running Scrum end to end at peak cadences of 9+ releases per month,
partnering with Product on roadmap and prioritisation, and setting the engineering
standards and code-review practices the team works to.
