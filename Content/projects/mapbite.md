A native iOS app I designed, built and shipped independently end to end — product design,
architecture, development, App Store distribution and ongoing maintenance, owned solo.

Save restaurants, cafés and bars to a personal map. Private places sync across devices
through the user's own iCloud account; a shared list syncs in real time between two
connected partners. Navigation hands off to Waze, Google Maps or Apple Maps.
